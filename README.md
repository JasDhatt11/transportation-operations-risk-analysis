# Transportation Operations & Risk Analysis

## About the Project

I built this project to look at transportation operations data and understand what patterns were showing up around incidents and operational risk.

I worked with a simulated dataset of 1,000 records and 33 variables. I used Python and Pandas to clean the data, explore the information, and look for patterns that could be useful from an operations and risk-management perspective.

> **Data note:** This is a synthetic dataset created for portfolio and learning purposes. It does not contain confidential information from a real transportation company.

## Project Questions

I wanted to answer a few practical questions:

- What types of incidents are happening most often?
- Are there certain operational areas that appear to have more risk?
- Are there noticeable patterns across vehicles, routes, or other factors?
- What areas could be monitored more closely?
- What could an operations team do to reduce potential risk?

## Tools

- Python
- Pandas
- Excel
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## Dataset

The dataset contains **1,000 records and 33 variables** representing simulated transportation operations and incident information.

The data was created specifically for analysis and portfolio development.

## What I Did

I started by reviewing and cleaning the dataset, checking the different variables and looking for missing or inconsistent information.

I then explored the incident and operational data to identify patterns and areas that stood out. From there, I translated the findings into practical observations and recommendations that could be useful to an operations or risk team.

## Areas I Looked At

- Incident frequency and patterns
- Operational risk
- Vehicle-related information
- Route and location patterns
- Operational performance
- Potential areas for improvement

## Recommendations

Some of the actions I would consider based on the analysis include:

- Keep track of recurring incident patterns rather than looking at incidents individually.
- Pay closer attention to areas showing consistently higher risk.
- Use historical incident data when planning preventive measures.
- Improve consistency in operational reporting and documentation.
- Review the data regularly to identify new or emerging patterns.

## What I Learned

This project gave me an opportunity to work with a larger operational dataset and practice the full analysis process — from cleaning the data to finding patterns and turning those findings into practical recommendations.

It also helped me think about data beyond just numbers and look at how operational information can be used to support better decisions.
